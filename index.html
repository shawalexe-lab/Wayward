01 — Wayward

No one asks where your road began. Only where you're going next.

A company of the Horde

Wayward is built for players who want something more personal than another name above their character and something less rigid than a guild that tells them how they have to play.

We dungeon. We explore. We gather, craft, fight, trade, roleplay and help one another get where we're going. Different people will find different reasons to stay. The company is what connects them.

You don't have to arrive with a story already written. You don't have to fit someone else's mold. What matters is showing up, contributing and becoming someone the people beside you can count on.

Everyone carries something.

What we carry, we carry together.
02 — The Company

Many roads.
One company.

Wayward isn't built around titles. Authority stays simple, responsibility stays visible, and most of us stand together as the Company.

I

Waylord

One voice at the head of the Company. The Waylord sets its direction, represents Wayward and carries final responsibility for where the road leads.

II

Hands

A small circle trusted to act for the Waylord and look after the Company. Hands help lead events, welcome new members, manage shared resources and keep Wayward moving.

III

Company

The body of Wayward. Full members who have made their place among us and can be counted on to contribute, participate and stand beside the people carrying the same mark.

IV

Wayfarers

New arrivals traveling with Wayward. A Wayfarer isn't beneath the Company; they're finding their place within it. Show up, take part and the distinction doesn't last long.

The road inward

You arrive a Wayfarer. You become Company by becoming someone the Company would notice missing.

03 — The Share

Everyone carries something. Everyone draws from what we carry.

The Share is Wayward's common store: a small contribution from the Company, gathered so the Company can provide for its own.

Coin
50 silver / week
A small weekly contribution from every full member of the Company.
Materials
One stack / week
One full stack from the current needs list. Requested materials change with the needs of the Company.
Wayfarers
No Share owed
New arrivals owe nothing while finding their place. The Share begins when a Wayfarer becomes Company.
Where it goes

The Share isn't payment to Wayward's leadership. Coin and materials placed in the guild bank exist to serve the people contributing to it.

What the Company gathers can be turned back toward whatever helps the Company move forward.

Crafting Consumables Equipment Events Professions Member aid
No debts

Miss a week? Speak to a Hand. There are no fines, no accumulating back-pay and no ledger of debts. The Share exists to strengthen the Company, not price someone out of it.

The bank belongs to no one. What enters it belongs to Wayward.

The Company provides because the Company contributes.
04 — The Mark

Some things are recognized. Others are given.

The Wayward Mark

A lantern. A feather. A road still ahead.

The lantern is Wayward's mark: something carried into the dark, not a promise that the road ahead will be easy.

Beneath it hangs a worn feather. Weathered. Imperfect. Still moving. No two roads leave the same marks on the people who walk them.

The Feather

When a Wayfarer becomes Company, a Ruffled Feather is given to them. It is ordinary by design. Its worth comes from the people who understand what it means.

You don't carry the mark because you joined. You carry it because you stayed.

Given by a Hand. Carried by the Company.
